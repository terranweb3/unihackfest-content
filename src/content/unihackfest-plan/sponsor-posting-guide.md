# Sponsor Posting Guide

This guide is for MEXC Ventures, Solana, and other sponsors with track, prize, ecosystem, or technical involvement.

## Role in the Campaign

Sponsors provide ecosystem credibility, technical direction, and post-event opportunity. Sponsor content should explain why the sponsor supports student builders and how its track or resources help teams build better products.

## Core Message

> Sponsors help UniHackfest connect student builders with real market insight, technical resources, ecosystem exposure, and opportunities beyond Demo Day.

## Recommended Content Types

- Sponsor announcement
- Track explainer
- Prize / support announcement
- Technical resource post
- Mentor / judge announcement
- Finalist or winner shoutout
- Post-event impact post

## Track-Specific Angles

| Sponsor Type | Content Angle |
|---|---|
| Venture / market sponsor | Market insight, digital asset products, GTM, user research |
| Technical ecosystem sponsor | Developer resources, devnet, tooling, dApp quality, technical judging |
| Education partner sponsor | Learning resources, student readiness, skill development |
| Prize sponsor | Incentives, recognition, next-step opportunities |

## Tone

Professional, ecosystem-oriented, founder-friendly, builder-focused, concise.

## Sample Post

**Title:** Supporting student builders at UniHackfest 2026

We are proud to support UniHackfest 2026, a 14-week AI & Web3 learning and competition journey for university students in Vietnam.

Through this program, student teams will move from market insight and product thinking to technical execution, demos, and Demo Day.

We look forward to supporting the next generation of builders as they turn ideas into real products.

## Assets Needed

- Sponsor logo and usage guideline
- Track description
- Prize or support details
- Technical resources link
- Quote from sponsor representative
- Co-post checklist
- Winner congratulations template
