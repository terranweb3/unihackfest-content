# Weekly Posting Frequency

### Normal Weeks

| Channel | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Corelia Academy | 2–3 posts / week |
| UniHackfest | 3–5 posts / week |
| University / Faculty | 1 post / major milestone |
| University Clubs | 2–4 posts around campus events |
| Sponsors | 1–2 posts / phase |
| Mentors / Judges | 1 personal post before or after participation |
| Student Ambassadors | 1–2 posts / week during active phases |

### Peak Weeks

Peak weeks include Kick-off, campus workshop week, deadline week, Qualification Round, and Demo Day.

| Channel | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Corelia Academy | 3–5 posts / week |
| UniHackfest | Daily post or story |
| Clubs | Daily reminder / story near event day |
| Sponsors | 1–2 posts + reposts |
| Mentors / Judges | 1–2 posts |
| Student Ambassadors | 2–3 posts + stories |
