**Prepared for:** Corelia Academy  
**Event website:** https://unihackfest.vn/  
**Campaign role:** Corelia-led multi-stakeholder content campaign  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-05-28

---

## 1. Strategic Context

UniHackfest 2026 should be communicated as a **14-week student builder journey**, not simply as a one-day hackathon.

The current event positioning on the website is:

> **Learn. Build. Ship.**  
> From campus to real AI & Web3 products, in 14 weeks.

UniHackfest is positioned as a **learning + competition program for university students in Vietnam**, helping teams move from **market insight** and **AI product thinking** to building **Web3 products they can demo**.

Key proof points from the website:

- **$10,000** total prize pool value
- **15 universities** participating
- **2,000+ students** participating
- **5 hands-on courses**
- Organized by **Corelia Academy × UEF**
- Sponsored by **MEXC Ventures** and **Solana**
- Education & ecosystem partners include **She Blockchain**, **OpenCampus**, and **Commandoss**
- Final Demo Day planned at **SIHUB**

---

## 2. Communication Objective

### Primary Objective

Build awareness, trust, and registration momentum for UniHackfest 2026 through a coordinated content campaign led by Corelia Academy and amplified by universities, clubs, sponsors, mentors, judges, speakers, partners, and student ambassadors.

### Secondary Objective

Position Corelia Academy as the education and execution engine behind a national student AI & Web3 builder movement.

### Core Communication Idea

> UniHackfest 2026 is where Vietnamese university students learn, build, and ship real AI & Web3 products — from campus workshops to Demo Day.

---

## 3. Main Message Architecture

### Master Message

UniHackfest 2026 is a 14-week AI & Web3 learning and hackathon journey for university students in Vietnam, helping students go from market insight and product thinking to real demos, prototypes, and builder opportunities.

### Supporting Messages

#### For Students

You do not need to be a Web3 expert to start. UniHackfest gives you the roadmap, courses, mentors, team environment, and Demo Day platform to build your first real AI & Web3 product.

#### For Universities

UniHackfest helps universities bring practical AI, Web3, product thinking, and innovation experience to students through a structured, partner-supported program.

#### For Sponsors

UniHackfest gives ecosystem sponsors a direct channel to educate, activate, and support the next generation of student builders in Vietnam.

#### For Mentors / Judges / Speakers

UniHackfest creates a meaningful platform for experts to guide student teams from raw ideas to real prototypes and public demos.

#### For Partners

UniHackfest is a collaborative student builder movement that combines education, hackathon execution, university activation, and ecosystem support.

## 17. Final Recommendation

Corelia should run UniHackfest as a **multi-voice campaign** instead of a single-page announcement campaign.

The strongest structure is:

- **Corelia Academy** builds credibility and strategic narrative.
- **UniHackfest Official** drives action, registration, deadlines, and student guidance.
- **Universities** provide academic legitimacy.
- **Clubs** create student-level trust and participation.
- **Sponsors** provide ecosystem credibility and technical direction.
- **Mentors / Judges / Speakers** provide expert validation.
- **Student Ambassadors** create peer-to-peer momentum.

The campaign should consistently reinforce one message:

> **Learn. Build. Ship. From campus to real AI & Web3 products, in 14 weeks.**
