# KPI Framework

## Awareness KPIs

- Total reach
- Total impressions
- Website visits
- Social follower growth
- Video views
- Event page visits

## Engagement KPIs

- Reactions
- Comments
- Shares
- Saves
- Story mentions
- Partner reposts
- Student ambassador posts

## Conversion KPIs

- Registration clicks
- Completed registrations
- Number of students registered
- Number of teams registered
- Number of universities represented
- Bootcamp attendance rate
- Course completion rate

## Community KPIs

- Number of university posts
- Number of club posts
- Number of sponsor posts
- Number of mentor / judge posts
- Number of student-generated posts
- Number of Telegram / community members

## Competition KPIs

- Number of submitted projects
- Number of qualified teams
- Number of finalists
- Number of demos completed
- Number of winner projects
- Number of projects continuing after Demo Day

## Partner KPIs

- Sponsor impressions
- Sponsor engagement
- Track participation
- Partner leads generated
- University collaboration opportunities
- Post-event sponsor satisfaction
