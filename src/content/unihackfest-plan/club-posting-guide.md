# Club Posting Guide

This guide is for university clubs, student communities, student ambassadors, and campus organizers.

## Role in the Campaign

Clubs create peer-to-peer trust and turn the campaign into something students feel they can actually join. Club content should lower the barrier to entry, make the event feel energetic, and help students find teammates.

## Core Message

> You can join UniHackfest even if you are new to Web3. Start from the workshop, learn through bootcamp, find teammates, and build your first real product.

## Recommended Content Types

- Campus workshop invitation
- “Why students should join” post
- Team matching / looking-for-teammates post
- Reminder posts before workshop day
- Behind-the-scenes stories
- Photo recap
- Student quote cards
- Learning progress and build log posts

## Posting Flow for Campus Workshop

| Timing | Content | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| T-7 days | Workshop announcement | Save the date |
| T-5 days | Why join this workshop | Register |
| T-3 days | Speaker / agenda reveal | Invite friends |
| T-1 day | Final reminder | Attend |
| Event day | Check-in stories | Follow live |
| T+1 day | Photo recap | Tag friends |
| T+3 days | Next step post | Join bootcamp / register |

## Tone

Friendly, energetic, direct, student-level, and practical.

## Sample Post

**Title:** Looking for your first AI & Web3 hackathon?

UniHackfest 2026 is coming to our campus.

Join the workshop to understand the roadmap, meet other students, find teammates, and learn how to start building your first AI & Web3 product.

You do not need to be an expert to begin. Come learn, ask questions, and explore the journey from idea to Demo Day.

## Assets Needed

- Campus poster
- Story countdown template
- Check-in frame
- Team matching template
- Speaker cards
- Photo recap template
- Short FAQ for students
