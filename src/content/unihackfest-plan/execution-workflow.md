# Execution Workflow

## Weekly Workflow

1. Confirm campaign phase and priority CTA.
2. Select content pieces for Corelia, UniHackfest, clubs, partners, and sponsors.
3. Prepare captions and visuals in one shared content kit.
4. Send partner-specific assets at least 3–5 days before posting date.
5. Track publishing status in a shared sheet.
6. Collect screenshots / links after posting.
7. Measure performance weekly.
8. Adjust next week’s content based on registration and engagement data.

## Recommended Tracking Sheet Columns

- Date
- Phase
- Content title
- Owner
- Channel
- Format
- Caption status
- Visual status
- Approval status
- Scheduled date
- Published link
- Reach
- Engagement
- Clicks
- Notes
