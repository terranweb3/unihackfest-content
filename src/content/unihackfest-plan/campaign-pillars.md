# Campaign Pillars

## Pillar 1 — Learn Before You Compete

Focus: Courses, learning path, workshops, bootcamp, Cora AI Student Plan.

Key message:

> UniHackfest is not just a competition. Students first build a solid foundation through structured courses and guided learning.

Recommended content:

- Course roadmap explainer
- C1–C5 learning path carousel
- Learning checklist for students
- “No Web3 background? Start here” post
- Cora AI Student Plan announcement
- Bootcamp reminder posts

---

## Pillar 2 — Build Real AI & Web3 Products

Focus: Product thinking, MVP, Solana devnet, AI product layer, market insight.

Key message:

> Students will not only learn theory; they will build tangible products that can be demoed.

Recommended content:

- “From idea to MVP” guide
- Product idea prompts
- Technical build checklist
- Solana dApp devnet explainer
- AI product MVP examples
- Team progress updates

---

## Pillar 3 — Ship and Showcase

Focus: Qualification Round, Demo Day, pitch deck, final pitching, awards.

Key message:

> UniHackfest gives students a real stage to present their products to mentors, judges, sponsors, investors, and ecosystem partners.

Recommended content:

- Demo Day announcement
- Finalist announcement
- Pitching tips
- Judging criteria explainer
- Winner announcement
- Project spotlight series

---

## Pillar 4 — National Student Builder Movement

Focus: 15 universities, 2,000+ students, campus workshops, student communities.

Key message:

> UniHackfest connects students across universities into one national builder journey.

Recommended content:

- Campus tour announcement
- University spotlight
- Club collaboration posts
- Student ambassador posts
- Photo recap from each campus
- “15 campuses, one builder journey” content

---

## Pillar 5 — Ecosystem and Career Pathways

Focus: MEXC Ventures, Solana, OpenCampus, She Blockchain, Commandoss, mentors, judges, incubation, partnership opportunities.

Key message:

> UniHackfest connects student builders with real ecosystem partners, technical resources, career exposure, and post-event opportunities.

Recommended content:

- Sponsor spotlight
- Track announcement
- Mentor spotlight
- Judge announcement
- Partner appreciation
- Post-event incubation / next-step posts
