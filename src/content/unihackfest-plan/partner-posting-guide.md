# Partner Posting Guide

This guide is for education partners, ecosystem partners, communities, media partners, and supporting organizations such as She Blockchain, OpenCampus, and Commandoss.

## Role in the Campaign

Partners help UniHackfest reach relevant communities, strengthen trust, and show that the program is connected to a wider AI & Web3 ecosystem. Partner content should explain the shared mission and invite communities to participate.

## Core Message

> UniHackfest brings universities, students, mentors, sponsors, and ecosystem partners together to support the next generation of AI & Web3 student builders in Vietnam.

## Recommended Content Types

- Partnership announcement
- Community invitation
- Learning opportunity post
- Resource sharing post
- Speaker / mentor support post
- Recap or appreciation post
- Student builder spotlight

## Posting Moments

| Moment | Recommended Post | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Partnership launch | Partnership announcement | Follow UniHackfest |
| Before workshops | Community invitation | Register / join |
| During bootcamp | Learning resource post | Start learning |
| Before Demo Day | Support finalist teams | Follow Demo Day |
| After event | Appreciation / impact post | Continue collaboration |

## Tone

Supportive, community-oriented, mission-driven, inclusive, and practical.

## Sample Post

**Title:** Partnering to support student builders

We are excited to support UniHackfest 2026, a national learning and competition journey for university students exploring AI, Web3, product thinking, and builder skills.

By joining forces with universities, mentors, sponsors, and ecosystem partners, UniHackfest creates a practical environment where students can learn, build, and showcase real products.

We invite our community to follow the journey and support the student builders.

## Assets Needed

- Partner logo
- Partnership boilerplate
- Community invitation caption
- Approved event description
- Recap photo set
- Quote from partner representative
- Social repost guidelines
