# University Posting Guide

This guide is for UEF, university faculties, departments, and official university pages.

## Role in the Campaign

University channels provide academic legitimacy, official reach, and trust for students and parents. Their content should make UniHackfest feel like a credible learning and career development opportunity, not only an extracurricular competition.

## Core Message

> UniHackfest 2026 gives students a structured opportunity to learn AI, Web3, product thinking, teamwork, and pitching through a practical 14-week journey.

## Recommended Content Types

- Official collaboration announcement
- Campus workshop announcement
- Faculty-supported student opportunity post
- UniTour recap after each campus session
- Student achievement / shortlisted team announcement
- Congratulations post for finalists and winners
- Post-event impact summary

## Posting Moments

| Moment | Recommended Post | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Before launch | Collaboration announcement | Follow official updates |
| Before campus workshop | Workshop invitation | Register / attend |
| After workshop | Photo recap | Join the next phase |
| Bootcamp period | Learning opportunity post | Start learning |
| Qualification | Support selected teams | Cheer / follow |
| Demo Day | Finalist support post | Follow Demo Day |
| After event | Impact and congratulations | Continue collaboration |

## Tone

Formal, credible, student-development focused, clear, and institutionally appropriate.

## Sample Post

**Title:** Students join UniHackfest 2026

Through UniHackfest 2026, students have the opportunity to explore AI, Web3, product thinking, teamwork, and pitching through a structured learning and competition journey.

The program connects students with workshops, bootcamp learning, mentors, ecosystem partners, and Demo Day, helping students turn ideas into real product demos.

Students are encouraged to follow UniHackfest updates and join upcoming activities.

## Assets Needed

- Official event logo
- University / faculty logo
- Campus workshop poster
- Speaker / mentor profile cards
- Registration link or QR code
- Recap photo album
- Approved boilerplate about UniHackfest
