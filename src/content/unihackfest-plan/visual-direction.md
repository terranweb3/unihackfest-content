# Visual Direction

## Core Visual Style

- Bright university innovation style
- Clean, modern, student-friendly
- AI + Web3 + campus energy
- Blue, cyan, purple, green gradients
- Mascot-friendly when targeting students
- More formal layouts for sponsors and university partners

## Recommended Visual Formats

- 1:1 square posts for Facebook / LinkedIn
- 4:5 portrait carousels for higher feed presence
- 16:9 banners for website, event pages, and YouTube
- 9:16 stories for countdowns and live updates
- Quote cards for mentors, judges, students
- Infographics for roadmap, tracks, and impact numbers
