# Campaign Timeline

## Phase 1 — Pre-Launch / Awareness

**Timing:** Late May 2026 – Early June 2026  
**Goal:** Build initial awareness and explain what UniHackfest is.

### Key Messages

- UniHackfest is a 14-week AI & Web3 learning + competition journey.
- The program helps students learn, build, and ship real products.
- The campaign is backed by universities, Corelia Academy, UEF, MEXC Ventures, Solana, and ecosystem partners.

### Content Checklist

| Content | Owner | Format | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Official launch announcement | Corelia + UniHackfest | LinkedIn / Facebook post | Follow for updates |
| What is UniHackfest? | UniHackfest | Carousel | Read roadmap |
| Why students should join | Corelia | Carousel / article | Join waitlist |
| 14-week roadmap overview | UniHackfest | Infographic | Explore roadmap |
| Sponsor announcement | Corelia + Sponsors | Co-post | Follow sponsors |
| University collaboration announcement | Corelia + UEF | LinkedIn post | Stay updated |

## Phase 3 — UniTour: 15 Campuses

**Timing:** June 2026 – July 2026  
**Goal:** Activate universities and convert awareness into student participation.

### Key Messages

- UniHackfest is coming to 15 campuses.
- Students can learn Web3, Solana, and shape hackathon ideas through campus workshops.
- Each campus is part of a national student builder journey.

### Standard Content Flow for Each Campus

| Timing | Content | Owner | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-7 days | Campus stop announcement | Corelia + Faculty + Club | Poster |
| T-5 days | Why join this workshop | Club | Short post |
| T-3 days | Speaker / mentor / agenda reveal | UniHackfest + Club | Single image |
| T-1 day | Final reminder | Club + UniHackfest | Story / post |
| Event day | Live check-in / behind the scenes | Corelia + Club | Story / reel |
| T+1 day | Photo recap | Corelia + Club | Album |
| T+3 days | Next step: join Bootcamp / register | UniHackfest | CTA post |

### Suggested Campus Recap Structure

1. Thank the university and club.
2. Mention student participation and workshop focus.
3. Highlight what students learned.
4. Connect the workshop to the next UniHackfest phase.
5. Include CTA for registration / Bootcamp / follow channels.
