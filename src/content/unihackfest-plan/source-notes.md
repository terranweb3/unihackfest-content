# Source Notes

This plan was prepared based on the public UniHackfest website as of 2026-05-28:

- https://unihackfest.vn/
- The website positions UniHackfest as a 14-week AI & Web3 learning and competition program for university students in Vietnam.
- The website lists Corelia Academy × UEF as organizers, MEXC Ventures and Solana as sponsors, and She Blockchain, OpenCampus, and Commandoss as education & ecosystem partners.
- The website outlines the 5-stage roadmap: Official Kick-off, UniTour across 15 campuses, Bootcamp Learning, Qualification Round, and Demo Day at SIHUB.
